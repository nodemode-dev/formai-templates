# Code of conduct

formai-templates is where the Form apps' templates and demos are gathered and where the maintainers review each copy
in the open. This page says how we treat each other while doing that. It applies to this repository, its issues,
pull requests and review threads, and to anyone speaking for it elsewhere.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, with people new to the Form apps above all.
- Review the change, never the person: point to the file and the line, and say what would pass.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise. A failed check is a fix to make, not an accusation.
- Accept correction gracefully, and give it kindly and specifically.
- Credit the work of others: the photographers, musicians, type designers and authors whose material the demos carry.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct, on any grounds.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption of any kind.
- Adding material you have no right to share, or removing its credit.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code by opening an
[issue](https://github.com/nodemode-dev/formai-templates/issues), or by reaching the maintainers on GitHub: the
[nodemode-dev](https://github.com/nodemode-dev) organisation, [@nodemode-ai](https://github.com/nodemode-ai) and
[@gbarnai](https://github.com/gbarnai). A harmful file is a security matter: report it privately as
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says, so it can be removed before it is discussed.

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and decide what is fair: a private word, a public note, removal of content, or a
temporary or permanent ban from the repository's spaces. People who report in good faith will not face retaliation.
